Reply with a single JSON object. Fill every field:

- score: fluency 0-100 for THIS turn. Score what the learner actually said, not the effort behind it.
    Start from 100 and deduct: 12 for each grammar error a teacher would correct (wrong tense, missing or wrong article, wrong word form); 8 for wording no native speaker would use even though it is understandable; 6 for each clearly mispronounced word; 10 if the sentence is incomplete or you had to guess the meaning.
    Bands: 95-100 nothing at all to correct. 85-94 natural with at most one very small slip. 70-84 a full sentence with one real error. 50-69 several errors, or understandable only from context. Below 50 not a sentence, or barely intelligible.

    Calibration - score these exactly as shown. Where your arithmetic disagrees with these examples, follow the examples:

STAYING IN THE SCENE:
